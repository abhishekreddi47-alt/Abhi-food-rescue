🍱 Food Rescue & Donation

📌 About the Project

Food Rescue is a simple web-based platform that helps reduce food waste by connecting people or restaurants with NGOs and volunteers.

🎯 Objective

- Reduce food wastage
- Help people in need
- Connect food donors with volunteers
- Make food donation simple and easy

✨ Features

- 🍱 Donate surplus food
- 🤝 Connect donors and volunteers
- 📍 Share food pickup details
- 📋 View available food donations
- 📞 Contact information
- 📱 Simple and user-friendly interface

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

🚀 How It Works

1. A person or restaurant adds available surplus food.
2. A volunteer or NGO can view the donation.
3. The volunteer collects the food.
4. The food is delivered to people who need it.

🌱 Social Impact

The project helps reduce food waste and supports people who need food by making the donation process easier.
